1.1 adds Qwen3-ASR as a speech model option (downloaded by you, runs entirely on your Mac).

• Settings → Speech Model → Catalog download: choose Qwen3-ASR 1.7B or 0.6B alongside Whisper and switch any time
• In our tests, short dictation is noticeably faster than Whisper large-v3-turbo (results vary by Mac)
• Your custom dictionary is passed to Qwen as recognition hints
• If a Qwen model cannot load, the app falls back to Whisper and explains why in Settings

Qwen3-ASR is released by the Alibaba Qwen team under Apache-2.0. It runs on your Mac; no recording or text is uploaded. The only network connection is still downloading model files from Hugging Face.
