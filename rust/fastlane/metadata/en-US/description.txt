lailaisay is a menu-bar dictation tool. Hold a hotkey, speak, and release: Whisper transcribes on your Mac, the text lands on the clipboard, and ⌘V pastes it into any app.

Features
• Fully offline speech recognition: whisper.cpp with Metal acceleration on Apple Silicon. Audio never leaves your computer.
• Built for Traditional Chinese: Taiwan-style characters, punctuation, number and list shaping, filler-word removal. English and mixed-language input work too.
• Custom dictionary and phonetic corrections for names, products and jargon.
• Choose your model, from tiny to large-v3-turbo.
• Configurable hotkey with hold-to-talk and double-tap lock.

How it works
1. Pick and download a speech model on first launch
2. Allow the microphone
3. Hold ⌘⇧Space, speak, release
4. Press ⌘V in any text field

Privacy
Recognition runs entirely on your Mac. No recording and no text is ever sent to any AI service; the only network connection this app makes is downloading speech model files from Hugging Face.
